Grishin Aleksei — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how the games published by Grishin Aleksei (“we”, “us”) handle your data. It applies to the following apps:

  • Bread2Go
  • Avrora
  • Catch

The general rules below apply to every app. What each app collects is described in its own section under Apps.

Who we are

The apps are developed and published by Grishin Aleksei, an independent developer based in Japan. Contact: lyoshagrishin98@gmail.com.

General principles

  • We collect only the data an app needs to work, as described in its section.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, or Google account inside our apps.
  • Game progress is stored on your device unless an app's section says otherwise.

Third-party services

Some apps use the services below. Each app's section lists exactly which ones it uses.

  • Google Play — distributes the apps and processes in-app purchases. We never see or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) — confirms purchases and restores them if you reinstall an app. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Some of these providers are located outside Japan, including in the United States, so data sent to them may be processed there.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

How to request deletion — step by step:

  1. Send an email to lyoshagrishin98@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line [App name] data deletion, for example Bread2Go data deletion.
  3. Include the details listed in that app's section, so we can locate your record. Our apps have no accounts, so this is the only way to find it.
  4. We reply within 7 days to confirm, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we cannot delete:

  • Google Play transaction records. Google keeps these to meet tax and accounting obligations, typically for seven years. They are held by Google, not by us, and we have no ability to remove them.
  • Data on your device — this never reaches us; it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when an app adds a new service, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

Contact

lyoshagrishin98@gmail.com

Apps

Bread2Go

Bread2Go is a single-player game for Android. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics of its own.

What the game collects: nothing. Game progress is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

In-app purchase: the game offers one optional in-app purchase. Buying it is never required to play, and nothing in the game is locked behind it. It is processed by Google Play and confirmed by RevenueCat, which receives:

  • an anonymous identifier generated for your installation
  • your purchase history: product, transaction identifier, and its status

RevenueCat does not collect crash logs or diagnostics from this app, and the game contains no advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind. This information is not linked to your name, email, or Google account, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Deletion request: use the subject Bread2Go data deletion and include the date and approximate time of your purchase. The anonymous identifier and purchase history held by RevenueCat are deleted within 30 days. After deletion, your purchase can no longer be restored.

Avrora

Avrora is a game in development. In its current version it collects no data: it has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no in-app purchases, and any game progress is stored only on your device.

Before any feature that collects data is added, this section will be updated to list exactly what is collected and which services receive it.

Catch

Catch is a game for Android. It collects no data: it has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no in-app purchases, and any game progress is stored only on your device.

Before any feature that collects data is added, this section will be updated to list exactly what is collected and which services receive it.

Приложения

Bread2Go

Bread2Go — одиночная игра для Android. В ней нет аккаунтов, входа, рекламы и собственной аналитики.

Что собирает игра: ничего. Прогресс хранится только на вашем устройстве и удаляется вместе с приложением.

Встроенная покупка: в игре есть одна необязательная покупка. Она ничего не открывает и не требуется для прохождения. Платёж проводит Google Play, а подтверждает RevenueCat, который получает:

  • анонимный идентификатор, созданный для вашей установки
  • историю покупок: товар, идентификатор транзакции, статус

RevenueCat не собирает из этой игры отчёты о сбоях и диагностику, а рекламных и аналитических SDK в ней нет вообще. Эти данные не связаны с вашим именем, почтой или аккаунтом Google и не используются для рекламы.

Запрос на удаление: тема письма Bread2Go data deletion, укажите дату и примерное время покупки. Анонимный идентификатор и история покупок в RevenueCat удаляются в течение 30 дней. После удаления восстановить покупку будет невозможно.

Avrora

Avrora — игра в разработке. В текущей версии она не собирает никаких данных: в ней нет аккаунтов, рекламы, аналитики и встроенных покупок, а прогресс хранится только на вашем устройстве.

Прежде чем в игре появится функция, собирающая данные, этот раздел будет обновлён: в нём будет указано, что именно собирается и каким сервисам передаётся.

Catch

Catch — игра для Android. Она не собирает никаких данных: в ней нет аккаунтов, рекламы, аналитики и встроенных покупок, а прогресс хранится только на вашем устройстве.

Прежде чем в игре появится функция, собирающая данные, этот раздел будет обновлён: в нём будет указано, что именно собирается и каким сервисам передаётся.
